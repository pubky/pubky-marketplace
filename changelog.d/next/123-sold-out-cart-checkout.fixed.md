A sold-out listing now shows Sold out in place of Buy, and no longer offers Make offer. In the cart and at checkout, a line whose listing sold out reads Sold out, and a line whose size sold out reads This size is sold out; Checkout and Pay stay disabled with the reason until the buyer removes it. If the service refuses Pay (for example because the last unit sold a moment earlier), its reason now stays next to Pay instead of only flashing a toast.
