Messages were rebuilt. Each message is now an end-to-end encrypted file on your own homeserver, readable only with the chat key kept under your account's private storage (so your homeserver operator could read it; sealing that key is a planned follow-up). New messages arrive live through your homeserver's event stream and the Pubky inbox, and your conversations, unread counts and read state follow your account to every device and browser instead of staying on one. Messaging works with Bitkit, Pubky Ring and Passport sign-ins alike; a message to someone who has not opened Messages yet waits and is delivered once they do. Sessions approved before this change keep working and see "Messages need a newer sign-in approval" in Messages: sign out and sign in again to turn messages on. The local database is recreated on first launch after the update (version 9), which only clears cached data. Conversations from the earlier messaging beta were not carried over, since the app had not launched.
