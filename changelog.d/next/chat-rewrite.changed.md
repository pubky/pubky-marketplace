Messages were rebuilt. Each message is now an end-to-end encrypted file on your own homeserver that only you and the other person can read; no Shop service or operator sees it. New messages arrive live through your homeserver's event stream and the Pubky inbox, and your conversations, unread counts and read state follow your account to every device and browser instead of staying on one. Messaging works with Bitkit, Pubky Ring and Passport sign-ins alike; a message to someone who has not opened Messages yet waits and is delivered once they do. Sessions approved before this change see "Messages need a newer sign-in approval": sign out and sign in again to turn messages on. Conversations from the earlier messaging beta were not carried over, since the app had not launched.
