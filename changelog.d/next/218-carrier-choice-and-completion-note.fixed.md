The ship form no longer preselects USPS: the carrier select starts on "Select carrier" and the seller must pick one, so a seller outside the US can't record USPS or a USPS tracking link by leaving the default. Adding or editing tracking still starts on the carrier the shipment recorded. A delivered order that takes returns now says when it really completes, for example "Completes automatically 14 days after delivery unless a return is requested. A return can be requested within 30 days of delivery, even after the order completes.", instead of saying it completes after the return window (pubky-marketplace #218).
