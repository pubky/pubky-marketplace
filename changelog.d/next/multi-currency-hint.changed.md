The approximate "≈" hint beside a price now converts into the buyer's display currency (any of the eight listing currencies, chosen in the marketplace filter bar) instead of replacing the price on cards. Prices always show in the listing currency; the hint is indicative only and is hidden when the rate is unavailable. Rates come from the Blocktank bitcoin feed that Bitkit shows, read once per snapshot so fiat-to-fiat hints use two rates from the same moment.
