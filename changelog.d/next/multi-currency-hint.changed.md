The approximate "≈" hint beside a price now converts into the buyer's display currency (any of the eight listing currencies) instead of replacing the price on cards. The display currency starts from the browser's language (de-CH gives CHF, eurozone locales EUR, en-GB GBP, ja JPY, en-CA CAD, en-AU AUD, otherwise USD) until the buyer picks one in the marketplace filter bar, and their choice always wins. Prices always show in the listing currency; the hint is indicative only and is hidden when the rate is unavailable. Rates come from the Blocktank bitcoin feed that Bitkit shows, read once per snapshot so fiat-to-fiat hints use two rates from the same moment.
