Ratings count only counted reviews: verified purchases from an attestor this app trusts, signed inside its trust window. The storefront headline is the seller rating from shipping and communication ("4.7 seller rating · 3 buyers"), with item stars below it as "Products rated 4.6 across 2 listings · 3 buyers · 4 orders"; a seller whose reviews predate the seller questions reads "No seller rating yet", and one with nothing counted reads "No rating yet". Cards and the listing page's product rating show the per-buyer mean of trusted reviews with their buyers and orders. Review lists put counted reviews first and the rest under "n not counted", and check a trusted key's window against the attestation time once the index serves it. An index without trust buckets shows "No rating yet" in place of stars, because its average covers every indexed review.
