Imports with many photos now load every photo in one go, in batches, instead of needing a second import: the dry run says how many photos and batches there are, progress shows the batch, and when the photo service asks to wait the import waits, says so, and carries on. If it has to stop, Resume picks up with the photos that are left and nothing is published for those listings yet. If you close the import or reload the page part-way, opening the import again offers "Resume your unfinished import" with Resume and Discard, and Resume carries on with the photos that are left and then the publish, without loading a photo twice. The dry run also says roughly how often the photo service's allowance will make a large import pause.
