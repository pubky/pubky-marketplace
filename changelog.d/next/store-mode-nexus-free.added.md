Store mode reads no Nexus and no CDN: `PUBKY_RUNTIME_NEXUS_URL`, `PUBKY_RUNTIME_MARKETPLACE_NEXUS_URL` and `PUBKY_RUNTIME_CDN_URL` may be left unset and are ignored when set. Sign-in skips the social bootstrap, feeds and mutes; profiles and avatars come from each user's homeserver, and the shop, listings, seller dashboard and watchlist from the seller's homeserver. Community tags, reviews and ratings are not shown in a store yet. The grant sign-in accepts users of any homeserver, and Passport is offered only when the store sets `PUBKY_RUNTIME_PASSPORT_URL`.
