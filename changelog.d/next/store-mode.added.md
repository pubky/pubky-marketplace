Store mode: `PUBKY_RUNTIME_STORE_SELLER` runs the Shop as one seller's store. `/` opens that seller's shop; other sellers' pages, global browse and search, the social pages, the hosted Market's support and agent endpoints and files the app does not ship return 404, and only the store seller sees and can open the selling tools. For every other signed-in user, orders, offers, reviews and notifications are filtered to the buyer side, and sale chat threads are hidden and closed to writing and sending, in sandbox and live commerce modes alike. A store is never embedded or framed. A store opened over plain HTTP shows how to open it over HTTPS instead of a sign-in that cannot finish. Unset, the Shop is unchanged.
