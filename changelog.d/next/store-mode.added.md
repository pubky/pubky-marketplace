Store mode: `PUBKY_RUNTIME_STORE_SELLER` runs the Shop as one seller's store. `/` opens that seller's shop; other sellers' pages, global browse and search, the social pages and the hosted Market's support and agent endpoints return 404, and only the store seller can open the selling tools. Any other signed-in seller keeps only its buyer side there, in orders, offers, notifications and messages. A store is never embedded or framed. A store opened over plain HTTP shows how to open it over HTTPS instead of a sign-in that cannot finish. Unset, the Shop is unchanged.
