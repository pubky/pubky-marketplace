When the Bitcoin or USDT payment request's delivery reads "failed", the buyer is told "We couldn't confirm your wallet got the payment request. Check Bitkit for it; if it's not there, add the seller as a contact in Bitkit, then check out again.", not that the wallet didn't receive it. On an elapsed checkout the copy adds that the window ended and a request found in Bitkit must not be paid. The state can read failed after a crash near the deadline even though the request arrived; payment detection is unchanged.
