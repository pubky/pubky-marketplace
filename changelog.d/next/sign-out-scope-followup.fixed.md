A seller follow started by adding to the cart or watchlist, checking out, following a shop, making an offer or placing a bid now stops when the buyer signs out, as the one started from a chat message already did. A watchlist check that was still running at sign-out starts no new listing check and saves nothing, a retried review removal sends nothing more for the account that left, and a profile or feed refresh that starts while another account signs in over the current one, or while sign-out is still clearing the account, no longer saves the previous account's view into the device. Outstanding review removals stay with their owner through account changes. Opening an order no longer deletes a newer public review. When a withdrawal leaves a public copy, the author can check its address and contents from Orders and finish removing it with a homeserver file manager.
