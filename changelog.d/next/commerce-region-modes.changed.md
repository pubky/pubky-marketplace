Commerce can now be set per region with `PUBKY_RUNTIME_REGION_MODES` (`EU=blocked,GB=blocked,US=blocked`, modes `open`, `nuanced` and `blocked`), and each later legal outcome is a runtime-config change. In a blocked region the Market stays open to browse, and Buy, Offer, Bid, Add to cart, Checkout and Pay are replaced by one line with an info icon ("Buying and selling aren't available in your region yet."). The seller studio, Sell, drops, store connections and Get paid show "Selling isn't available in your region yet." with a way back to the Market, paid unlocks say so, and a shop can't be set to a blocked country. The visitor's country comes from the new `/api/marketplace/client-region` route (header set by `PUBKY_RUNTIME_CLIENT_REGION_HEADER`), asked only from Market pages and only while a region is blocked. With the setting unset, `PUBKY_RUNTIME_EU_FENCE_ENABLED` and `PUBKY_RUNTIME_DIGITAL_REGION_BLOCK_ENABLED` work exactly as before.
