Signed-in visitors can narrow the review list on listings and storefronts to reviews from people they follow, their network (two follow hops) or people they tagged with labels of their choice. The rating at the top of the page then shows the figure for those reviewers, for example "4.8 from 3 people you follow", or "No reviews from people you follow" when none match. Sorting still never changes that rating, and ratings on cards and snippets stay unfiltered.
