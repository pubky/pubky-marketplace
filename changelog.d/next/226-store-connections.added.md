Sellers can connect a WooCommerce or Shopify store under Store connections in My shop. A guide walks through the WooCommerce approval (or a pasted REST key) and the Shopify private app, shows the store's health, and lets the seller choose which store products to also publish on Pubky. Store connections are off until `PUBKY_RUNTIME_STORE_CONNECTIONS_ENABLED=true` (default `false`), so the Shop can deploy before the service connectors. Orders → Orders in your store shows which sales reached the store, with Retry for the ones that did not.
