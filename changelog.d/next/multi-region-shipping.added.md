Sellers can price shipping by destination. In Settings > Shipping, a seller saves shipping profiles: zones of countries with flat or per-item rates, delivery estimates and excluded countries. A listing then ships under a saved profile or under zones of its own, set in the listing form, and import settings can apply a profile to every imported listing. A product page shows where the item ships and the rate to the buyer's country. At checkout the buyer picks a rate per shipping group; the order carries the delivery country and the rate, and is pinned to the exact profile the buyer saw, so a seller who changes prices mid-checkout makes Pay refuse and re-quote instead of charging a different amount. Offers stay off for zone-priced listings. Listings priced with a single flat option are unchanged.
