My listings in the seller studio can now be filtered by state, format, checkout registration, store source (Shopify or WooCommerce) and stock, searched by title or SKU, and sorted by date, title, price, inventory or state from the column headers or a sort menu. Filter counts show what each option would list, the view is kept in the URL, long catalogs are paged 50 at a time, and a page of selected rows can be extended to every listing that matches. Register selected registers the chosen listings for checkout in one action and names any that failed.
