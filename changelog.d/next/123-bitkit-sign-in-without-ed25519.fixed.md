Sign-in and sign-up with Pubky Ring, Bitkit and Pubky Passport now work in browsers without Ed25519 in WebCrypto, such as Chrome 124, instead of showing only "Click to reload". In those browsers the sign-in key is created by the Pubky SDK instead of WebCrypto and saved in IndexedDB as bearer-equivalent material, readable by scripts on the Shop's origin; browsers with WebCrypto Ed25519 keep their non-extractable key.
